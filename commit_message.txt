swbr: a vcam cell — lamps on the strip, a menu on a click

vcam is a virtual webcam: it loops the real camera through a filter and
overlay chain and writes the result to a v4l2loopback device that browsers
see as a camera. It listens on a unix socket and answers in lines.

source=vcam asks that socket itself, the way source=cmus asks cmus, rather
than running vcam-ctl on a timer — so whether it is up, what it is sharing
and whether it is recording are facts rather than a guess at what a script
printed. One connection a tick gets `list` and `record info`, non-blocking,
on the tick the bar already has: a wedged vcam must not take the bar with
it. The cell is not there at all while vcam is not running.

Folded, a new slim mode: dots. Round lamps, one per thing that is on —
green while vcam runs, a second red one while it is recording. slim_color2
changes the red one.

Clicking the cell opens a menu in the panel under the bar — the same strip
of surface the calendar lives in, which already grows and already takes
clicks. The rows are built from what vcam just said, so only what makes
sense is offered: share a region, share a monitor by name (from sway's own
output list), stop sharing when something is, show or hide the webcam
overlay, move it on round the six positions, start or stop recording. A row
lights up under the pointer, and the menu stays open while you work through
it, because setting a share up is several of these in a row.

Doing is not asking: the menu runs vcam-ctl. Picking a region needs slurp
and sharing a whole monitor needs geometry and down-scaling it already gets
right. src_path= finds it when it is not on $PATH.

Also fixed, found on the way: a height change the resize throttle turned
away was turned away for good. tip_h had already moved, so the caller never
asked again and the panel stayed invisible — which is why a menu with seven
rows would not open where one with five had. The bar remembers the refused
fit and asks again next frame.

The build id now covers sw_theme.h as well as swbr.c.

swbr: tint busy workspace buttons, and make a failed alert audible

ws_cpu_wash=45 tints a workspace button in the load colour, more of it the
busier the workspace is. The two-pixel dot column was the only sign before and
was easy to miss across a whole screen; the dots stay as the scale, the tint
is what the row says at a glance. The focused workspace keeps its own colour:
that is a different question and must not become a matter of degree.

Measured offscreen at 0.9 and 3.9 cores: a busy button's mean colour distance
from the bar background goes from 22 to 85 and from 48 to 80, while idle
buttons and the focused one are unchanged.

Also in this release, from the battery alert work:

- alert_gain (35%) sets the loudness of the generated tone, which moves no
  mixer at all.
- alert_vol_min raises the mixer for the tone and puts it back, through
  alert_vol_get / alert_vol_set. The restore is the shell's own EXIT trap, so
  it survives the alert being killed part way through. Not exec'ing the player
  is load-bearing here: exec replaces the shell and takes the trap with it.
- A player that fails during an alert now reports it on the bar. It used to
  fail silently, because swbr is started by sway and its stderr goes nowhere.
- swbr --alert-test fires an alert now, prints the levels, gain and mixer
  floor, plays the tone and reports the player's exit status, and shows what
  alert_cmd would run at each level without running it.
- The shipped example contradicted itself: alert_cmd runs at a level and only
  at a level, so the `[ %c -le 1 ] && poweroff` guard could never be true with
  levels 30,20,10,5. The list is now 30,20,10,5,1.

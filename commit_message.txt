swbr: the vcam menu follows along, and its share rows toggle

Cycling the webcam position needed the menu closed and reopened between
every step. The menu was only rebuilt when a crude bitmask of the vcam state
changed — running, recording, how many shares, webcam present — and moving
an overlay changes none of those. So the row went on offering the move it
had already made, and pressing it again sent the same command. The change
check is a digest of everything the cell and the menu are drawn from now,
overlay rectangles included, so the row updates in place and five presses
walk the webcam round the frame.

The share rows are toggles. A share swbr starts gets an overlay id of its
own — the monitor's name, or "region" — so the row that started it is the
row that stops it: "share screen DP-1" becomes "stop sharing DP-1". The
separate stop row is gone; one remains for shares none of those rows
covers, started from the command line or elsewhere.

The cell is blue in the advanced example — its own colour beside docker's
green and the clock's cyan — and so is the running lamp on the strip. The
recording lamp stays red.
